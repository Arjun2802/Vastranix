# About Vastrenix

**Billing, Stock, and Dealer Orders — One Thread.**

---

## Who We Are & What We Build

**Vastrenix** is an integrated retail point-of-sale (POS), multi-location inventory, and wholesale supply-chain platform built exclusively for India’s apparel and garment trade. 

Whether you manage a busy retail counter in Surat's textile markets, an upscale boutique in South Mumbai, an ethnic-wear store in Chandni Chowk, or a wholesale manufacturing unit in Tirupur, Vastrenix brings your daily commerce into one cohesive, dependable thread.

---

## Why Vastrenix Exists

Generic billing software was never designed for the clothing business:
- Real clothing stores deal with complex variants: every garment comes in multiple sizes (S, M, L, XL, XXL or custom kids' sizes), distinct color swatches (Indigo, Mustard, Rust, Ivory), and varied fabric classifications (Chanderi, Pure Cotton, Linen, Banarasi Silk).
- Fabric retailers and wholesalers sell across distinct pricing tiers: a retail walk-in customer pays retail MRP, a regular boutique buyer gets wholesale pricing, and long-term registered dealers receive dedicated supply rates.
- Garment businesses do not operate out of a single room: stock is split between active showroom display racks and separate storage godowns, requiring instant stock transfer challans without discrepancies.
- Traditional stores maintain deep relationships through Customer Khata (store credit), needing accurate month-end statements sent directly via WhatsApp.

We built Vastrenix from the ground up to solve these exact realities — not as an adapted generic accounting tool, but as a purpose-crafted operating system for garment merchants.

## Core Pillars of the Platform

### 1. Rapid Counter Billing
Generate clean GST invoices in under 15 seconds. Supports barcode and 2D QR scanners, fast item search, multi-tender split payments (Cash, UPI, Card, Store Credit), thermal receipt printing (58mm / 80mm), and instant WhatsApp invoice delivery.

### 2. Multi-Location Stock Control
Manage real-time inventory across retail showroom counters and bulk godowns. Receive incoming supplier purchase orders (POs), conduct live audit stock counts, and execute instant inter-branch transfer challans.

### 3. Customer Khata & Dues Tracking
Eliminate lost slips and forgotten credit balances. Track customer receivables with clear transaction histories, record partial cash settlements, and send gentle month-end payment reminders via WhatsApp.

### 4. B2B Dealer Supply Network
Connect directly with authorized apparel wholesalers, mills, and fabric distributors. Exchange digital purchase orders, verify live catalog availability, and track order dispatches with zero phone miscommunication.

### 5. Enterprise Cloud Reliability
Powered by Google Cloud and Firebase architecture. Operates seamlessly offline with automatic cloud synchronization as soon as internet connectivity is restored.
